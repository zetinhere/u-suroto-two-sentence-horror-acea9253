One horror story of exactly two sentences (168 characters). A dying father in hospice answers yes or no by squeezing a hand — two squeezes for yes, one for no — until the narrator asks the one question that matters and gets an answer in a number the code does not have. The program validates the story against the gate (1–400 characters, exactly two sentences, the second sentence carrying the turn, no fingerprint of a known story) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

> The hospice nurse showed me how my father answers now: two squeezes for yes, one for no. I asked if the man in the bed was still my father, and he squeezed three times.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
